Output fidelity — does the recipe change what the model says?
  • Weights unchanged: NVIDIA's NVFP4 checkpoint as shipped; Spark/RTX banks are byte re-layouts, SHA-256 equal on both ends
  • Layers 1–12 vs stock vLLM on identical inputs (shadow, graphs + proxy + graphed workers): cosine mean 0.99979–0.99984, layer 12 0.99931
  • All 92 layers on full-model inputs (29,440 layer-tokens) vs an fp32 checkpoint reference: 87 ≥ 0.99, median 0.996, no cross-tier leaks
  • Layers 24/25/36/48 lose precision to NVFP4 activation quantization (0.84–0.99 vs fp32); both tiers match an exact emulation at 0.992–0.997, as does stock
  • Worker kernel non-deterministic: 0.8–2 % repeat spread (GB10), heavy tail to ~5–6 % (RTX sm_120); boot-to-boot logprob Δ median ~0.13
  • Teacher-forced divergence vs a reference engine — no machine here can hold the whole model
