## Honest read

- The drafter works mechanically. It loads as a drop-in on the incoai geometry, serves without errors, and gives sane decaying per-position counters. It is simply weaker than incoai on every distribution we have, our own included. The likeliest causes, in order:
  1. Data volume. One pass over 150K samples, against canada-quant's 736K, and P2's loss curve was still falling at the end (P2-RESULTS: −0.37 CE over the second half).
  2. The 0.5 KL weight and the loss port are our choices, not a reproduced recipe.
  3. Inco's training set and steps are unpublished and likely much larger.

  None of these were tested here; this card measures, it does not diagnose.
- The PLAN's premise was that "the only remaining lever for prose is a drafter trained on the thinking-off prose/agent mix we serve". That premise is not supported at this scale. Prose went from 2.34 (incoai) to 1.97 (ours), not toward 2.6.
- The secondary goal (an Apache-licensed drop-in) is met mechanically, at a cost of −16 to −19 % C1 prose tok/s and −0.4 accept on the cq kit vs incoai on this lane. Per PLAN.md, it is still publishable as a negative result.
- Lane validation is a side result worth keeping. Same-day GB300 incoai numbers reproduce the weekend Spark curve and canada-quant's card, which confirms again that acceptance belongs to the drafter, not the stack.
